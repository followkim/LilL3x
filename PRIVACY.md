**Privacy Policy for LilL3x**

We're committed to protecting your data and ensuring that your interactions with our service remain private. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and share your information.

**Data Collection**

When using the LilL3x service, you may be required to provide certain personal or device-related information. Here's what we collect:

Audio data is transmitted over the Internet to the selected speech-to-text engine for processing.
Text conversation data is sent over the Internet to the selected Large Language Model (LLM) for interaction and response generation.
API keys are shared with our service for authentication purposes.
Photos may be uploaded to Imgur, a third-party image-sharing platform, in order to share visual information with the LLM.

**Data Use**

We use the collected data solely for the purpose of providing a functional LilL3x experience. This includes:

Translating audio input into text using the selected speech-to-text engine
Processing text conversation inputs to generate responses using the selected LLM
Authenticating user requests and managing access through API keys

**Data Sharing**

We do not share your data with any third-party services or organizations, except for the following:

Speech-to-text engines and LLMs: We transmit audio and text data to these external services as necessary for processing.
Imgur: Photos may be uploaded to Imgur in order to share visual information with the LLM.

Email is used solely for authentication against our non-publicly available server, which is unselected by default.

**Data Security**

We take data security seriously and implement measures to protect your information:

Data transmission: Audio and text conversations are transmitted over secure protocols to prevent unauthorized access.


**Changes to this Policy**

Please note that we may update this policy periodically. We encourage you to review the policy regularly to stay informed about any changes.
